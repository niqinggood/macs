# Professional Handwritten Knowledge Poster

Use this reference when the output is a standalone poster, social long image, course recap, investment brief, meeting summary, market note, or method explainer that should look like an expert's real handwritten knowledge map.

## Format

- Default canvas: vertical 9:16.
- Physical surface: realistic off-white A3 paper or whiteboard notebook sheet on a desk.
- Camera feel: lightly photographed, natural paper texture, subtle shadows, slight edge curvature.
- Visual goal: professional, restrained, content-dense, warm, and shareable.

## Layout

Use a three-zone structure:

1. Top title zone
   - Main title.
   - One-line summary or judgment.
   - Small tag such as `重点一览`, `核心复盘`, `手写版`, `今日判断`.

2. Middle knowledge zone
   - 6 to 8 content modules.
   - Each module has a number, heading, and 2 to 5 short points.
   - Vary module size by importance; do not make every block identical.
   - Use clean hand-drawn borders, dividers, arrows, callouts, and section labels.

3. Bottom conclusion zone
   - One final judgment, action recommendation, formula, risk note, or next step.
   - Make this area visually stronger than ordinary modules so the poster has a clear landing point.

## Content Rules

- Preserve concrete facts, numbers, source-specific terms, risks, and conclusions.
- Convert long prose into short note-like points.
- Prefer practical judgments over abstract slogans.
- Include risks, tradeoffs, or action implications when the source supports them.
- Do not invent facts to make the poster look full.
- Avoid fake dense text. Every visible label should be intentional and readable.

## Visual Language

- Title: black thick marker handwriting.
- Body: black medium/fine marker handwriting, neat and readable.
- Accents: dark blue for emphasis, ink green for structure, muted red for risks, warm yellow for highlights.
- Keep colors to 4 or 5 total.
- Icons: use only restrained line icons such as lightning, rocket, chart, scale, checklist, warning mark, magnifier, check mark, arrow, light bulb, star.
- Icons must look hand-drawn, not 3D, flat-stock, or cartoon.

## Avoid

Do not create:

- Blue-purple tech gradients.
- Cyberpunk, glassmorphism, or futuristic chip backgrounds.
- 3D icons, cute cartoon illustrations, stickers, or scrapbook styling.
- Childish hand-copied classroom posters.
- Low-end marketing long images or generic public-account cards.
- Mechanical print-font layouts that look like PPT templates.
- Garbled text, typos, long English paragraphs, cluttered icons, excessive whitespace, or messy information hierarchy.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 professional handwritten knowledge poster on realistic off-white A3 paper.
It looks like an expert researcher, investment advisor, consultant, or course instructor carefully organized the source after reading it.

Top: {title}; one-line judgment: {summary}; small tag: {tag}.
Middle: 6-8 structured modules, each with a number, concise heading, 2-5 short points, restrained hand-drawn icons, clear hand-drawn borders, and varied module sizes.
Bottom: emphasized conclusion / action recommendation / risk note: {bottom conclusion}.

Style: real paper texture, natural shadows, professional marker handwriting, black title, readable black body text, dark blue emphasis, ink green structure, muted red risk marks, warm yellow highlights.
Avoid: tech gradient, cyberpunk, glassmorphism, 3D icons, cartoon scrapbook, childish poster, low-end marketing long image, fake text, typos, clutter.
```
