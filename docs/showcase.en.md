# macs screenshots and demos

[Back to the project](../README.en.md) · [简体中文](showcase.md) | English

These 8 screenshots come from the V1.1.8 feature demonstrations. They cover conversations, collaboration, Pipelines, knowledge retrieval, document automation, and memory. The examples use demo data and the Chinese interface. Click any image to open its original size.

## 1. Conversations and workbench

Manage conversations, select an Agent and model, and start work from task suggestions in one interface.

[![macs workbench](images/showcase/overview.png)](images/showcase/overview.png)

## 2. Multi-Agent expert teams

Select a research, coding, full-stack, content, or review team and organize collaboration around distinct roles.

[![Expert team templates](images/showcase/expert-teams.png)](images/showcase/expert-teams.png)

## 3. Pipeline templates

Select workflows by business scenario and inspect their steps, categories, and entry points.

[![Pipeline template library](images/showcase/pipelines.png)](images/showcase/pipelines.png)

## 4. Pipeline editor

Configure each step's instructions, Agent, and tool scope to turn a working method into a reusable process.

[![Pipeline step editor](images/showcase/pipeline-editor.png)](images/showcase/pipeline-editor.png)

## 5. Knowledge retrieval

The demo asks which documents an expense claim requires. The interface shows a matching answer and the source passage from the knowledge base.

[![Knowledge retrieval and source passage](images/showcase/knowledge.png)](images/showcase/knowledge.png)

## 6. Word field mapping

After importing a Word template and structured data, review field mappings, sample values, and match status alongside the template preview.

[![Word template field mapping](images/showcase/document-mapping.png)](images/showcase/document-mapping.png)

## 7. Word generation and reusable profiles

Preview and download the completed demo document, then reuse its mapping profile for matching templates.

[![Generated Word document and reusable profile](images/showcase/document-output.png)](images/showcase/document-output.png)

## 8. Memory management

Browse saved preferences and facts, search memories, and open records to inspect, correct, or manage them.

[![Memory list and search](images/showcase/memory.png)](images/showcase/memory.png)
